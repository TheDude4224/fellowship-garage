More Than a Car

This is about serving the community, mentoring others, and building something meaningful together.

The Fellowship Garage is a community-driven classic car restoration project that combines automotive passion, teamwork, creativity, and charitable impact. Together, we're restoring a classic vehicle while building friendships, learning hands-on skills, creating media content, and connecting with local businesses.

When the restoration is complete, the finished car will be used to raise money for Kingdom Builders — turning wrenches into real impact for our community.

📅 Every other Tuesday · 6:30–8:00 PM 📍 The Fellowship Garage · Davisburg, MI ⛪ Freedom Center Church · Fenton, MI

There's a place for you if you…

  • Love cars and want to turn wrenches
  • Enjoy photography, video, or social media
  • Want to help organize events and fundraisers
  • Can connect us with local businesses
  • Just want to be part of something bigger than yourself

More Than a Car

This is about serving the community, mentoring others, and building something meaningful together.

The Fellowship Garage is a community-driven classic car restoration project that combines automotive passion, teamwork, creativity, and charitable impact. Our project car is a 1949 Buick 4-door sedan — postwar Detroit steel from the golden era of the waterfall grille and the straight-eight. Together we're bringing it back to life while building friendships, learning hands-on skills, creating media content, and connecting with local businesses.

When the restoration is complete, the finished car will be used to raise money for Kingdom Builders — turning wrenches into real impact for our community.
